The USAJOBS recommendation below was not adopted.** No dataset file, sample CSV, Parquet partition or live posting endpoint was requested. No account/key was created or used. ## Decision

**Recommend USAJOBS Historic JOA plus Announcement Text for a bounded, explicitly federal-job-focused Bay Area study.** It has the clearest documented reuse position among the assessed options and a documented route to both structured records and original announcement text without waiting for API-key registration. This is the best permission-first option for a five-day attempt, not a promise of sufficient Bay Area postings in all four target roles.

This recommendation changes the sampling frame from a general employer/selected-commercial-board study to federal advertisements. Keep the nine counties, transparent four-role classification, advertised-pay rules and technical stack. Rename or subtitle the project to make the federal scope visible, for example **Bay Area Federal Data & ML Job Posting Intelligence**. Do not make this change without user approval. If broad private-sector coverage is non-negotiable, no assessed option is presently a fully verified replacement satisfying every original requirement.

An explicit published license or permission statement can authorize reuse; a separate provider email is not inherently necessary. The user's source approval and empirical suitability checks are separate from provider permission. A license badge alone is weaker evidence when the publisher does not establish rights in third-party content or the needed raw fields are absent.

## 1. USAJOBS: strongest documented rights and access

Official [terms, Public Data Use](https://developer.usajobs.gov/guides/terms-of-service) explicitly allow copying, storage, reformatting, analysis, publication and redistribution of defined Public Data without prior OPM approval. Marked legal/third-party restrictions remain exceptions. Identify the source/retrieval date and avoid implying government endorsement; inspect metadata exceptions and omit unnecessary contact details from public exports. These permissions cover relevant public endpoints, not applicant/private information.

The [Historic JOA reference](https://developer.usajobs.gov/api-reference/get-api-historicjoa) explicitly states no authentication and documents title, agency, locations, pay bounds/period, opening/closing dates and control number. It supports date/occupational-series filters and continuation-token pagination. The [Announcement Text reference](https://developer.usajobs.gov/api-reference/get-api-announcementtext) separately states no authentication and documents long-text fields including summary, duties, qualifications and education. The shared control number supports joining text and metadata. Follow continuation tokens to exhaustion; do not assume a single page is complete.

This resolves the earlier documentation-level authentication uncertainty in the Phase 1/2 notes. The ordinary current-job Search API still requires a key according to the [Job APIs overview](https://developer.usajobs.gov/api-reference/job-apis); it is not required merely because it belongs to the same service. The [rate guide](https://developer.usajobs.gov/guides/rate-limiting) labels its 500-row page/10,000-row query limits as Search limits; do not transplant those limits or its 'no limits' labels to Historic JOA/Announcement Text. No numeric historic request quota or access fee was established. Reachable docs are not a live-service test.

**Project assessment:** Best for retaining original text evidence and relational provenance. After approval, choose one fixed, completed calendar-year window, retrieve bounded relevant occupational-series metadata, filter explicit work locations to the nine counties, and obtain matching text only for candidates. Series codes are retrieval aids, not accepted role labels. Confirm available history, dates, text coverage, pay applicability and volume before promising any KPI. Five days cannot guarantee enough Data Analyst, Data Scientist, Data Engineer and Machine Learning Engineer advertisements; report sparse roles rather than relabeling unrelated jobs. Federal grades and telework eligibility also do not automatically establish seniority or hybrid arrangement.

## 2. Luke Barousse: fastest prepared historical alternative

The publisher's [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lukebarousse/data_jobs/blob/main/README.md) labels the CSV Apache-2.0 and describes 2023 postings with original title/location/company/date fields, derived salary averages, parsed work-from-home flags and extracted skills. Full descriptions, raw salary bounds, explicit pay provenance and source-native IDs are not established by the card. Local counts and role coverage were not inspected.

The [Apache-2.0 license](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) grants reproduction, derivative-work and distribution rights to the licensed work from authorized contributors. Preserve applicable license/attribution/NOTICE material and identify changes. This is meaningful reuse evidence, but not independent verification of the upstream rights in an aggregator-derived dataset.

**Project assessment:** A ready historical file reduces acquisition work. It would require approval to label results historical, use publisher-extracted skill tags rather than our own text extraction, and distinguish derived salary metrics from verified advertised annual-pay midpoints. Missing original evidence cannot be reconstructed from averages; the primary salary KPI may be unavailable. A false remote flag is not proof of on-site work. Do not assume a row is a canonical unique posting without identifiers. Freeze the exact revision and checksum after approval; no download was made here.

## 3. Headcount Labs: explicit database license, limited content rights

The publisher's [repository documentation](https://github.com/HeadcountLabs/job-postings) describes structured job/posting/location/company tables, source-versus-derived values, and a snapshot without description text. Its full dataset is described as private/coming soon, despite other wording that describes a full dataset on Hugging Face; only public subsets are an immediate claimed route. The Hugging Face organization/schema and separate GitHub license link could not be fetched here, so subset access/coverage remain unverified. No sample was opened and no publisher counts are adopted as our findings.

The [data license](https://headcountlabs.com/license/) permits database copying/adaptation/use under ODbL and licenses the publisher's own values under DbCL. Employer descriptions, third-party rights and personal details are expressly excluded. Attribution, applicable removal obligations and share-alike conditions must be respected. [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) distinguishes database rights from individual-content rights; public adapted databases and produced dashboard works have different obligations. Do not assume a Tableau extract is merely a picture.

The [hosted-service terms](https://headcountlabs.com/terms/) allow analytics/research integration subject to third-party rights. They do not supply the missing right to use employer descriptions; account/API access and the open dataset license are distinct. The [rate documentation](https://headcountlabs.com/docs/rate-limits/) advertises 100,000 records/month without a card, plus free-account request limits and page sizes. Those are documentation claims, not tested account entitlements. API key setup is required for the normal API; public MCP bulk copying is prohibited by the terms. No signup or API query was performed.

**Project assessment:** A useful metadata-only alternative, especially for relational design, but insufficient for original-text skills work. Do not scrape linked employer pages to replace excluded text. Derived pay/location fields also need provenance checks against our strict advertised-pay/geographic rules. Not the default five-day choice given release/access uncertainty and the content gap.

## 4. Sources not cleared by this review

[Greenhouse API docs](https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html) establish technical access; the [legal index](https://www.greenhouse.com/legal) does not establish this project's intended third-party rights. The [Adzuna terms](https://developer.adzuna.com/docs/terms_of_service) identify personal/academic research, attribution, evaluation access and organizational/commercial restrictions; intended storage/publication and account details remain unresolved. Neither is newly enabled.

Remotive's [API README](https://github.com/remotive-com/remote-jobs-api) permits specified attributed sharing, while its [general terms](https://remotive.com/terms-of-use) restrict reproduction/derivatives/redistribution. Arbeitnow's [terms](https://www.arbeitnow.com/terms) include an API backlink condition but restrict the general use license to transitory viewing and prohibit public display. No broad project-specific reuse clearance was found. Do not read an API endpoint or backlink condition as unlimited storage/publication permission.

A further publisher dataset, `scholarweave/Jobs-Dataset-v2`, appeared in search, but its standalone README/license could not be accessed through the research tool. It is not recommended without accessible license/provenance evidence; indexed schema snippets are not a substitute. BLS remains suitable supplementary employment/wage context, not a source of job descriptions or posting counts.

## Five-day decision and acceptance checkpoint

Recommended approval: **USAJOBS historical metadata + text, federal-focused nine-county Bay Area scope**, with a bounded snapshot window and transparent four-role candidate review. This keeps Python/SQL/SQLite, statistical reasoning, raw text evidence and Tableau while avoiding an unresolved commercial-source permission dependency. It changes the population being described, not the meaning of 'job posting' or 'actual hire'.

Day 1 of the accelerated plan must be a real acquisition/profiling checkpoint after approval: verify service access, complete pagination, ID joins, data/text/pay coverage and plausible Bay Area counts. If those fail or a target role is sparse, present the evidence and seek a scope/time decision; do not claim market-wide insights or silently substitute a different source. Days 2–5 remain the existing planned phase sequence, subject to actual effort and separate phase gates. Approval of a source does not authorize Phase 3 in this task.

For a five-day portfolio, avoid running multiple live sources, waiting for new employer permissions, implementing a refresh service, or promising a historical trend merely because date fields exist. Preserve a versioned permitted snapshot and work from it. No promised sample size, salary statistic, skill trend or successful API result appears in this review.
